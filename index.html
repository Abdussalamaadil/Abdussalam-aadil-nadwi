About Abdus Salam Aadil Nadwi

Abdus Salam Aadil Nadwi is a religious and modern-education professional, author, editor and social & educational worker. His work focuses on education, moral development, guidance of young people, respect between teachers and students, and constructive social engagement.

He is the Director of Sir Syed Academy, Jokihat, and is associated with several educational, religious and social organizations in Araria district, Bihar.

“Education is not merely a means of earning a livelihood; it is a means of building a better human being and a responsible society.”
